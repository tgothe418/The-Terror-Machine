# Prohibited Invented Character Names & Architectural Spec

This document serves as the architectural spec for prohibited names during character generation in The Terror Machine.

## Rule: Canon Wins
The ban applies strictly to **invented** names generated during scenario authoring or synthesis. Where a name exists in authentic source material (e.g. historical records, canonical literary adaptations, or user-authored cast seed), **canon wins** and the ban does not apply (`fromSource: true`).

## Guiding Principle
Prefer plain, regionally plausible, slightly unglamorous names. Dale Brennan is scarier than Elias Crowe precisely because Dale Brennan could be real.
